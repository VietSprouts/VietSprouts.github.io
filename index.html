Viet Sprouts makes short, funny cartoons that teach Vietnamese and English to kids aged 4 to 10.

Our first series, The Tone Family, follows Bé O, his family and six friendly characters who are the six Vietnamese tones. Every word appears on screen with its tone mark, and kids are invited to say it along.

We teach Southern Vietnamese (miền Nam), with Huế words from Mệ (miền Trung).
