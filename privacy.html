Privacy policy

Viet Sprouts · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Viet Sprouts makes educational cartoons for children aged 4 to 10. We take children's privacy seriously. This page explains what information our website and our tools use, and what they do not.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no comments, no advertising, no cookies and no analytics. We do not collect any personal information from visitors, including children. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our videos on YouTube

Our videos are published on YouTube and marked as made for kids. We do not collect personal information from viewers. When you watch on YouTube, YouTube and Google handle your data under the Google Privacy Policy; YouTube's use is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Viet Sprouts uploader and other Google tools

"Viet Sprouts uploader" is a private tool used only by the Viet Sprouts team, signed in to our own Google account. It uses Google APIs for these purposes only:

  • Google Drive (permission drive.file): it uploads our own finished videos and review files into a folder it creates in our own Drive. It can only see files it created itself; it cannot see, read or change anything else in a Drive.
  • YouTube API Services (if and when we connect them): to upload and manage videos on our own Viet Sprouts channel only. By using any feature that relies on YouTube API Services you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any information about other people. The sign-in it uses is kept only in our private tool settings, never in public code. Information received from Google APIs is used only to run these uploads and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use it for advertising and we do not use it to train AI models.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections (or the Google security settings page). Removing access stops the tool at once; files already uploaded stay in the Drive of the account that owns them, and can be deleted there.

Children

Our content is for children, but we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, on this site, in our videos, or through our tools.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: transumenri@gmail.com.
